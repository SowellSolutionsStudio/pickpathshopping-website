You know your store. Now your list can, too.

Shopping should be the fun part. PickPath helps keep the little details in order so you can focus on getting what you came for.

  2. 2Shop and teach. Learning Mode remembers the item placements you record.
  3. 3Follow your PickPath. On later visits, known items appear in the order you've learned.
