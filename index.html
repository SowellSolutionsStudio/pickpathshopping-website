How PickPath works

You know your store. Now your list can, too.

  1. 1Choose your store. Build a shopping list for where you're headed.
  2. 2Shop and teach. Learning Mode records item placements during your trip.
  3. 3Follow your PickPath. On later visits, known items appear in your learned store order.
